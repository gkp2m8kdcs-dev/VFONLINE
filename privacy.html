Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Overview

VFONLINE ("we," "our," or "us") is built with a local-first philosophy. Your portfolio, notes, watchlist, learning progress, and attached images are stored only on your device. We do not run a backend, we do not sync to a server, and we do not sell your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information VFONLINE stores, how permissions are used, and your rights as a user.

Data We Store Locally

All data lives exclusively on your iPhone in the secure iOS sandbox. The following categories may be saved:

  • Portfolio holdings you add or import
  • Watchlist symbols and price alert thresholds
  • Investment notes, tags, and color categories
  • Image attachments stored in the app sandbox
  • Calculator preferences (risk-free rate, decimals, theme)
  • Learning progress and quiz scores

Permissions We Request

VFONLINE requests only the permissions needed to deliver its features:

  • Camera — to capture investment notes and OCR screenshots
  • Photo Library — to attach existing images to your notes
  • Photo Library Add — to optionally export generated charts

How We Use These Permissions

  • Camera and photo access are gated behind your explicit action. No image is uploaded anywhere.
  • OCR runs entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. Detected text never leaves your phone.
  • If you choose to reset the app, all data is wiped from local storage in one tap.

Third-Party Services

  • VFONLINE does not include third-party analytics, ad networks, or crash reporters.
  • No account is required and no email is collected.
  • We do not request or store your Apple ID or iCloud credentials.

Children's Privacy

VFONLINE is intended for adult investors and does not target children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on the device-level protections you have enabled (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, etc.). We recommend keeping your device secured with a strong passcode and the latest version of iOS.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data at all times. You can view, edit, export, or permanently delete every piece of information stored in VFONLINE directly from within the app.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever introduce optional cloud features, this policy will be updated and you will be prompted to consent before any data leaves the device.
