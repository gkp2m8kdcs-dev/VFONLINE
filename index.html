About VFONLINE

Welcome to VFONLINE — your global markets toolkit built for serious investors. Track portfolios, analyze risk, study markets, and capture investment ideas, all from one beautifully designed app.

  • Curated universe of 50+ global stocks with live charts and technical overlays
  • Portfolio tracker with diversification scores, Sharpe ratios, and sector allocation
  • Five investor-grade calculators: compound, position sizing, options, dividends, currency
  • Eight structured investing courses with progress tracking and quizzes
  • Investment journal with image attachments, tags, and on-device OCR
  • 100% local storage — your data never leaves your device

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All portfolio, notes, watchlist, and learning data is stored locally on your device. VFONLINE does not require an account and does not sync to any server.

How do I reset the app?

Open the Profile tab, go to Settings, and choose "Reset App Data". This will permanently remove all locally saved information in one tap.

Does VFONLINE provide investment advice?

No. VFONLINE is an educational and analytical tool. It does not provide personalised investment advice and does not transmit trades.

Why does the app request camera and photo access?

Camera access lets you scan investment notes and capture screenshots for OCR symbol detection. Photo Library access lets you attach existing images to your notes. Both are gated behind your explicit action and nothing is ever uploaded.

Does the app include ads or tracking?

No. VFONLINE contains no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking SDKs.
